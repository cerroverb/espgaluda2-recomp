Espgaluda II Black Label Recomp (Linux x86-64)

1. Copy your extracted Xbox 360 game files (default.xex, dat*.xex, res, ...)
   into the game_data folder.
2. Run espgaluda2-recomp.sh. For Steam, add it as a non-Steam game.

Press F4 in game for settings. Saves and settings go to user_data.
In Steam Game Mode, turn on Allow Tearing (quick menu, Performance) for no vsync.
